01 / INTENT

The motion is not the message.
The motion reveals the message.

Instead of glossy AI particles and dashboard theatrics, InkBrush Motion uses one restrained metaphor: a calligraphy brush moving through thought. Each stroke earns its place by clarifying sequence, hierarchy, or change.
